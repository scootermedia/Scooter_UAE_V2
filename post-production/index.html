About post-production with Scooter.

Why choose a post-production partner in India?

You get a senior edit, colour and sound team at a lower cost, working in a time zone that turns work around overnight for Gulf agencies.

How do you receive footage and share reviews?

Send footage by cloud transfer or drive. We share cuts through online review links with frame-accurate comments, so your team approves remotely.

Can you deliver in Arabic and other languages?

Yes. We handle subtitles, dubbing and multilingual versions, and deliver every aspect ratio your media plan needs.

Do you only edit footage you shot?

No. Most post-production work comes from footage shot by agencies and other production companies in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Qatar.
