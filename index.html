Questions we get asked.

Is Scooter a video production company in Dubai?

Yes. Scooter is a video production company in Dubai, with production hubs in Mumbai, Kolkata and Vancouver, serving brands and agencies across the Middle East and worldwide.

Do you work with agencies in the Middle East?

Yes. We produce for agencies, brands and real estate companies in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar and the wider GCC — shooting in the Gulf or in India, with post-production in India.

Can Scooter be our post-production partner in India?

Yes. Send us your footage and we handle edit, colour, sound, VFX, multilingual versions and every delivery format, with remote review for your team.

Do you make AI films?

Yes. Scooter AI is an AI production partner for brands — concept, characters, generation and finishing, directed by a production team on set since 2013.
