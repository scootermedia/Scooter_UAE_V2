The production backbone for agencies and brands.

The “this is real” moment wasn’t a single job. It was the first time a client came back for a second film, and then a third.

Today we produce live action across India and abroad, animation, motion design and CGI, and AI-driven brand films, from Dubai, Mumbai, Kolkata and Vancouver.

Meet the founder

Richaa Agarwal

Richaa founded Scooter in 2013 with one edit system, a few brave collaborators and a conviction that India could host world-class productions for global brands. She has since built it into a production partner for brands and agencies, with teams in Dubai, Mumbai, Kolkata and Vancouver, and films for names like Google, H&M Group, Warner Bros. Discovery and PUMA.

“I started Scooter with one edit system and a stubborn idea: that world-class films could be made from here, for anyone, anywhere. Thirteen years on, the kit has changed and the cities have multiplied. The idea hasn’t.”
