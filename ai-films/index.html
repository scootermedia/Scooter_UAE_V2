What does an AI production partner do?

We take a brief from concept to final film using AI — script, characters, world design, generation, edit, sound and languages — with the same craft and review process as a live-action shoot.

Can AI films work for regulated categories like pharma?

Yes. Our Dr Reddy’s brand film was produced entirely with AI and built to run in front of doctors.

Do you combine AI with live action?

Yes. We shoot what must be real and generate what can’t be, then finish both together in post-production.
